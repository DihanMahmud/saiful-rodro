# Homepage Information Architecture — Saiful Islam Studio
**Designed by:** Information Architecture Specialist · SaaS Agency Consultant
**Goal:** Maximize consultation bookings from non-technical business buyers
**Audience:** Small Businesses · eCommerce · Facebook Businesses · SMEs · Startups
**Reference benchmark:** Premium SaaS agency sites (Stripe, Linear, Mercury, Pilot, Clay, Bottega)

---

## Design Principles (apply across every section)

1. **One page, one job.** The entire homepage has a single conversion objective: book a free consultation. Every section exists to move a visitor closer to that action.
2. **Above the fold answers three questions in 5 seconds:** (1) Is this for me? (2) Can they deliver? (3) What's the next step?
3. **Trust compounds.** Trust signals are layered throughout — never concentrated in a single block. A new visitor should encounter proof every 2 scroll screens.
4. **Reduce, then animate.** Subtle motion guides the eye; it never competes with the message. Restrict animation to fade-up, marquee, and one hero entrance.
5. **One CTA copy across the entire site:** "Book a free strategy call." Repeating the exact phrase across hero, sticky bar, services, contact, and footer builds muscle memory and lifts conversion.

## Visual & Brand System (Premium SaaS Agency Aesthetic)

### Color palette direction
- **Primary background:** warm off-white (#FAFAF7) or deep charcoal (#0E0F12) — pick one and commit
- **Surface:** subtle off-white tint or near-black elevated card (#16181C)
- **Primary text:** near-black (#0E0F12) or near-white (#FAFAF7)
- **Secondary text:** muted neutral (#5A5D63 or #9CA3AF)
- **Accent:** one refined accent — calm teal (#0F766E), warm sand (#D6B884), or muted electric blue (#3D5AFE). Avoid neon greens, hot pinks, or anything that reads "crypto-bro."
- **Borders:** 1px, low-opacity (rgba black 8% or white 10%) — never heavy black lines
- **Shadows:** soft, layered (two shadow layers for depth), not harsh

### Typography
- **Display font:** a refined sans (Inter, GT America, Söhne, General Sans) or a contemporary serif (Fraunces, Instrument Serif, Tiempos)
- **Body font:** same family, regular weight
- **Scale:** Display 64–80px · H1 48–56px · H2 32–40px · H3 24px · Body 17–18px · Small 14px
- **Line-height:** 1.5 for body, 1.1 for display
- **Letter-spacing:** tight on display (-0.02em), normal on body

### Layout grid
- **Container:** max-w-7xl (1280px), 12-column grid, 24px gutters
- **Section vertical padding:** 96–160px desktop, 64–96px mobile
- **Reading width for prose:** max-w-prose (~70ch) for any paragraph longer than 3 lines
- **Card padding:** 32px desktop, 24px mobile

### Animation principles
- **Entrance:** fade-up 16px over 500–700ms ease-out, optional 50–100ms stagger
- **Hover:** 200ms transitions on transform and color, never on layout
- **Numbers:** count-up animation when scrolled into view (1.5s)
- **Marquee:** slow infinite loop (40–60s), pause on hover
- **What to NEVER use:** typewriter effects, particle systems, glow orbs, rotation on hover, parallax backgrounds, loading spinners on text, "AI" sparkle decorations

### Photography & imagery

## Conversion Path Summary

A visitor's path to booking a call has **4 decision gates**:

1. **Gate 1 — Recognition (Hero + Audience cards):** "Is this for me?" → Answer: yes.
2. **Gate 2 — Capability (Trust bar + Process + Case studies):** "Can they deliver?" → Answer: yes.
3. **Gate 3 — Fit (Services + Pricing + FAQ):** "Is this within my budget and timeline?" → Answer: yes.
4. **Gate 4 — Confidence (Testimonials + Final CTA):** "Should I act now?" → Answer: book a call.

Every section above exists to clear one of these four gates. Sections that don't clear a gate should be removed.
